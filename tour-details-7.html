Liwa Full Day Desert Drive

Overview

Experience the vast landscapes of Liwa on a full-day desert drive through the edge of the Empty Quarter. Visit a camel farm, stop for desert photos, enjoy lunch at an oasis, explore Liwa Fort, take in the sunset, and see Moreeb Dune, a remarkable large sand dune in the Liwa region. Price: AED 400 per person, with a minimum of 4 people per booking.

Tour Highlights

  • Enjoy a scenic desert drive through Liwa

  • Visit a camel farm and stop for desert photos

  • Have lunch at an oasis and visit Liwa Fort

  • Enjoy the sunset and discover Moreeb Dune

Tour Schedule

This is a full-day desert drive. Please confirm the pickup time and total duration when booking.

Who is it for?

Ideal for travellers who want to explore Liwa’s desert landscapes, local heritage, oasis scenery, and the impressive Moreeb Dune.

Tour Stops

  • Desert drive through Liwa

  • Camel farm visit

  • Desert photo stop

  • Lunch in an oasis

  • Liwa Fort visit

  • Sunset point

  • Moreeb Dune in the Empty Quarter desert

Important Notes

  • Minimum booking: 4 people.

  • Lunch at an oasis is listed as part of the tour itinerary.

  • Please confirm pickup time, duration, and any specific inclusions when booking.
